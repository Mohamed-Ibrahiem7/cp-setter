You have two boxes, $A$ and $B$, both initially empty.

The following process is performed for exactly $n$ days. On each day, one coin is added to one of the two boxes. The coin is added to box $A$ with probability $\frac{1}{2}$ and to box $B$ with probability $\frac{1}{2}$, independently of all previous days.

At most once during the entire process, immediately after that day's coin is added, you may perform the following operation:

\begin{itemize}
\item Choose one of the two boxes.
\item If the chosen box currently contains $x$ coins, replace the number of coins in it with $\left\lfloor \frac{x}{2} \right\rfloor$.
\end{itemize}

You may also choose not to perform the operation at all.

Let $a_i$ and $b_i$ denote the numbers of coins in boxes $A$ and $B$, respectively, after day $i$. These values are considered after both the coin addition and the optional operation on that day.

Your score is the number of days $i$ such that $a_i > b_i$.

Your decision whether to perform the operation, and which box to choose if you perform it, may depend on all coin placements observed up to that moment.

Assuming that you play optimally, determine the maximum possible expected value of your score.
