Amr and Zezo are attending a competitive programming training camp in Cairo. After the official contest ends, their coach, Fegla, prepares one final challenge.

There are $N$ problem sets. The $i$-th set initially contains $C_i$ unsolved problems and is worth $V_i$ points.

Amr moves first. On each turn, the current player must choose a problem set containing at least one unsolved problem and solve \textbf{exactly one} problem from it. If the player solves the \textbf{last remaining problem} in that set, they win the set, score $V_i$ points, and \textbf{immediately play another turn}. A player may receive multiple consecutive extra turns in this way. Otherwise, their turn ends and the other player plays next.

The game ends when all problem sets have been completed. Both players play optimally to maximize their own total score.

Find the maximum total score Amr can obtain.
